Lucian_5📖 read 14 ch. ★ 5/5Review
I really enjoyed this run, especially since I’ve only recently gotten into DC Comics. I think it does a fantastic job of presenting Batman as a genuine superhero rather than just a dark, brooding vigilante. One of my favorite moments was how he dealt with Killer Croc by sitting down and talking to him instead of immediately resorting to his fists. It’s such a simple but effective scene that perfectly captures what I love about this portrayal of Batman. Compared to some of the darker, more antihero-like interpretations, such as Frank Miller’s, this version reminds us that beneath the intimidating exterior, Bruce is ultimately a kind-hearted man who genuinely wants to help people. He understands that not every problem can be solved through violence, and sometimes compassion is far more powerful than any punch.

I also absolutely LOVE the new suit. The combination of blue and gray creates a beautiful contrast that really makes Batman pop on the page, and I hope we get to see more of this design in the future. The artwork is another major highlight, with the vibrant colors and dynamic visuals giving Gotham a distinct personality. Everything feels energetic and visually engaging without losing that classic Batman atmosphere.

Another detail I really appreciated was the inclusion of those little explanations for each gadget Batman uses. It might seem like a minor addition, but I think it does a wonderful job of showcasing just how intelligent, resourceful, and creative Bruce is. Batman has always been one of DC’s most fascinating characters because, despite having no superpowers, he can stand alongside some of the most powerful heroes in the universe through his intelligence, preparation, and sheer determination. Seeing the thought and engineering behind his equipment makes that aspect of his character even more satisfying.

I also loved seeing members of the Bat-Family appear throughout the story. Their presence reinforces the idea that Batman isn't just a vigilante fighting crime every night; he's also a human being who has built meaningful relationships with people he deeply cares about. I think that aspect of Bruce is incredibly important because it shows that, despite his reputation for being emotionally distant, he isn't someone who has completely shut himself off from the world. His relationships remind us that there's a person underneath the cowl, someone who needs other people just as much as they need him.

That said, I do wish we had seen Nightwing make an appearance. I know Dick is busy fighting crime in Blüdhaven, but he's my favorite DC hero, so I'm always hoping for more of him whenever the Bat-Family is involved. Seeing him interact with Bruce would've been a great addition, especially considering how interesting their relationship can be.

Overall, I think this is a fantastic run and a great introduction to DC's main Batman series for newer readers like myself. It balances action, humor, heart, and detective work while reminding us that Batman doesn't have to be the darkest character in the room to be compelling. If anything, I think his compassion is what makes him truly heroic. I completely agree with the 4.4 star rating, and I'm excited to see where the writers go with batman next. Batman is, without a doubt, a TRUE BLUE HERO.
